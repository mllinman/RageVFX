## 📏 Quality Standards

Every video must meet:
- ✅ Technical specifications (1080p+, 30fps+, H.264)
- ✅ Audio quality (-3dB normalized, noise-free)
- ✅ Clear narration (professional, paced)
- ✅ Accurate demonstrations (all steps work)
- ✅ Helpful annotations (arrows, highlights)
- ✅ Consistent branding (RageVFX style)

Every screenshot must have:
- ✅ High resolution (native or higher)
- ✅ Clear visibility (text readable)
- ✅ Proper annotations (when needed)
- ✅ Optimized file size
- ✅ Descriptive filename
